CapCut's free plan is real, but "free" depends on which assets and export settings you use. The plan itself costs nothing; the restrictions show up when a project touches Pro material or a Pro export setting.

Everything below comes from CapCut's public pricing page and export help page, checked on October 20, 2026. Prices change by region and over time, so verify before subscribing.

## What the free plan includes

CapCut's pricing page lists a $0 free plan with basic editing tools and free templates. In practice, a standard project using free assets can be exported at up to 1080p without a watermark.

That last point is the one people miss. CapCut does not add a watermark to every free export. It adds one when the project uses Pro-badged material, or on mobile when you decline the Pro prompt for a project beyond the free limits.

## When the watermark appears

Per CapCut's export help page, a clean export requires Pro when the project contains any of the following:

- Pro templates or template scenes
- Pro effects, transitions, or LUTs
- Pro fonts and text styles
- Pro music tracks or sound effects
- Pro stickers and overlays
- AI tools or AI-generated clips

One Pro asset is enough to trigger the watermark for the whole video. Resolution does not help: CapCut says watermark removal for videos using Pro material always requires Pro.

Some templates also include a CapCut-branded ending clip. That is an outro, not a watermark. Delete the clip at the end of the timeline and re-export. Our [watermark removal guide](/guides/remove-capcut-watermark) walks through the full check.

## What is locked behind Pro

The pricing page and help center point to these gates:

- **4K and 60fps desktop exports.** Both are Pro-only. Free desktop exports stop at 1080p.
- **Mobile length.** The free mobile limit is around 15 minutes and varies by region, according to the export help page.
- **Pro assets.** Templates, effects, fonts, music, stickers, AI tools, and premium transitions require a subscription for a clean export.
- **Cloud and AI extras.** Pro includes 1 TB of storage and 1,200 monthly AI credits on the US pricing page.

## What Pro costs

On the US pricing page checked October 20, 2026:

- **Pro:** $19.99 per month, or $179.99 per year
- **Ultra:** $79 per month, or $900 per year, with a larger AI credit allowance
- **Teams:** per-seat pricing, starting around $12.49 per seat per month for two seats

The displayed price can change with promotions and region. CapCut's page itself notes that displayed pricing is for reference and the final price appears after sign-in.

## What the $79 Ultra tier actually buys

Ultra is not a bigger editor. The comparison table on CapCut's US pricing page shows the same editing features and the same premium templates on Pro and Ultra. The difference sits in generation limits and storage:

| Plan | Monthly price | AI credits / month | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | none listed | — |
| Pro | $19.99 | 1,200 | 1 TB |
| Ultra | $79 | 8,645 | 1 TB |
| Teams | $12.49 per seat | 1,200 per seat | — |

If you edit ordinary videos, Pro already removes the watermark and unlocks 4K. Ultra is for people generating a lot of AI video or image assets — roughly seven times the credits for roughly four times the price. Paying for Ultra to "edit better" does not match what the page sells.

## Who is fine on the free plan

You can stay on CapCut free if:

- your videos are short, vertical, and edited with free templates and effects
- 1080p is enough for the platform you publish to
- you do not rely on Pro music, fonts, or AI features
- you check for Pro badges before export

For a weekly TikTok or Reels workflow built from free assets, the free plan is genuinely usable.

## Who should switch

Switch if any of these describes you:

- **You need 4K or 60fps.** DaVinci Resolve's free version supports up to 4K at 60fps and does not watermark exports, though it has a much steeper learning curve.
- **You need a guaranteed clean 1080p export from a browser.** Clipchamp's free tier includes 1080p exports with no watermark.
- **You want open source and local control.** OpenCut is MIT-licensed and free, but it is still early beta.
- **You keep using Pro templates and do not want to subscribe.** Rebuilding the same edit in Canva or Clipchamp is usually faster than removing every Pro asset one by one.

## How to check your own export

Before uploading, verify four things:

1. No Pro badge remains in the timeline.
2. Any template ending clip is deleted.
3. Export resolution is 1080p or lower.
4. The file is clean at the start, middle, and end.

If a logo survives those checks, isolate it by exporting a short section of the project. A Pro asset somewhere in the timeline can watermark an export even when the visible frame looks free.
